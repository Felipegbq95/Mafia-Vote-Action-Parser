Rules

You are free to discuss whatever you want, claim whatever you want, and draw whatever conclusions you want; just remember that anything can be faked. Once you are dead, you may only post in the thread for dead players, so do not continue to post in your faction's thread or in the main post. Keep communication with players that are alive to a minimum so you do not give anybody an unfair advantage. If you post as a dead player in this thread or your alignment's thread, your post(s) will be deleted, and your alignment will be punished as a result.

Communicating with other players, outside of the game thread about the game is forbidden.
Quoting/Screenshotting/using anything provided by moderators in a PM/Discord as evidence is forbidden, this includes but is not limited to providing a link to your forum.
Editing of posts after 10 minutes is forbidden.
Editing votes is forbidden.
Deleting posts is forbidden.
Players who do not post once in a Game Day will be mod-killed for inactivity barring certain circumstances. This does not apply if the Game Day lasts less than 24 hours.
Do not assume a Character's Allegiance based on their faction from other mediums. You may or may not be sorely disappointed.


New Addition

Although everyone will know who is playing from the sign-up list. Everyone will be assigned accounts for anonymity. It is highly encouraged that discussions stay more on topic related to the game rather than figuring out who is who or using who you are as a defense. However, this will not be enforced by mods.

When a card is flipped. It will show the account name on the card and not the actual player.


Questions/FAQs


If you have a question, you can always ask in the game thread. This will be considered to be part of the game and it will be up to the other players if they wish to reply or not. I will only reply if the question matters to all (i.e. I have forgotten to write a deadline in the morning post).

You may also ask a question in your Discord thread. I may not be able to answer based on the nature of the question.

All role cards upon death will have any links to other players in the game removed.


Day

Everyone has one vote per day to use to decide who to lynch. You can always change your vote, or even have no current vote. Once a player has gotten a majority of the alive players' total votes, they are immediately lynched and may no longer post in the thread - if you have information you need to make public, make sure you say it before you get that last vote. The day will go on until someone reaches the required number of votes for a lynch, the town decides to no-lynch or the deadline is reached.


Put your decision in Bold Letters
To vote for someone, use "Vote: [Player]"
To remove your vote, use "Unvote"
To vote for a no-lynch, "use VOTE: No-Lynch"


A vote that is not bolded will not be counted. I will accept whatever your most current vote is.

The day phase will typically last for 24 hours and will start at 8:00 PM EST and will shorten late game. Day 1 will be 36~ hours.

New Players
Clicking on a player's name will take you to their profile where you can click on show posts and easily see their post history.
You can easily navigate to the thread by clicking the Show unread posts since last visit button in the top right corner of the forums.
The https://www.mafiauniverse.com/wiki/Category:Roles or https://wiki.mafiascum.net/index.php?title=Main_Page is a good resource to learn common terms. I recommend reading the play advice section for your role. If you cannot find your role I can point you in the closest direction.
https://wiki.mafiascum.net/index.php?title=How_to_Not_Mess_Up_as_Town


Night

Everyone with a night action may post their action in their designated channels. No players are allowed to discuss anything in the game thread at this time unless specified. The Night Phase typically will last for 24 hours. The thread will not be locked in this game.




Day 1 will begin Monday and end Tuesday at 8pm eastern.

Before we begin the tournament to crown the winners of the world cup, we will be hosting a mini penalty shootout.

Each player will be given 1 vote that they cannot use on themselves.

At the end of the day, the top 4 most voted for players will participate in a penalty shootout during Night 1. Standard voting rules apply in relation to tied votes.

The Showdown will function as follows.

Match 1 - 1st vs 4th
Match 2 - 2nd vs 3rd

Match 3 - Loser of Match 1 vs Loser of Match 2
Match 4 - Winner of Match 1 vs Winner of Match 2

This will determine who places 1st to 4th.


How does the Penalty Shootout work?

Players take turns as the Shooter and the Goalkeeper, guessing one of three directions: Left, Middle, or Right.

To Score: The Shooter and GK must pick different directions.
To Save: The GK must pick the same direction as the Shooter.

Match Structure

3 Rounds: Each round consists of both players taking one shot and defending one shot.

Sudden Death: If it is still tied after 3 rounds, we move to sudden death (first to score while the other misses wins)

Quick Example:

Turn A: Player 1 (GK) dives Left. Player 4 (Shooter) aims Right. Will result in a goal (Player 4 leads 1-0)
Turn B: They swap roles. Player 4 (GK) dives Middle. Player 1 (Shooter) aims Middle. Will result in a save.
End of Round 1: Player 4 leads 1-0.

The 4 players chosen for the shootout will not be targetable by night actions

The day will end Tuesday @ 8pm est and the shootout will begin.
